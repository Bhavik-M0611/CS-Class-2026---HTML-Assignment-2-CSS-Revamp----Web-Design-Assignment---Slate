Build to Order

Every SlateBook is assembled to your exact specifications in our hardware lab. Because we use standard M.2 2280 storage slots and socketed LPCAMM2 memory modules instead of glue and solder, you can order a base configuration today and upgrade individual components yourself whenever your workflow demands more power.


How Custom Assembly Works

  1. Select Your Chassis & Silicon: Choose between the passive-cooled SlateBook 13, the Portable Pro 14, or the Performance-Oriented Pro 16 workstation.
  2. Configure Modular Memory & Storage: Pick your factory-installed (or bring your own) LPCAMM2 RAM kit and PCIe NVMe SSD capacity.
  3. Choose Your Clean OS Image: Select a zero-bloatware OS installation or order a blank drive to install your own OS for maximum control.
  4. 30-Minute Thermal Stress Test: Every custom machine undergoes a continuous 30-minute stress test before shipping to ensure reliability.
