Hardware-Enforced Privacy & Local AI

Physical Kill-Switches

Dedicated switches milled into the side of the chassis physically disconnect power to the webcam, microphone array, and wireless radio. No software bug or spyware can override an open circuit.

Super Low Resource Consumption

We have optimized our Slate OS and custom Windows 11 Pro - Slate Edition images to use less than 1.2 - 2.1GB of RAM at idle, with zero background telemetry or tracking services running. This means more resources for your workflows and lower power consumption.
